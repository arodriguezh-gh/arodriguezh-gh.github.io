Who I Am

I am a multidisciplinary engineer and problem solver working at the intersection of technology, data, organizations, and social impact.

I am currently completing a Master of Development Engineering at UC Berkeley, where I am learning to apply AI, data analytics, big data, and emerging technologies to development challenges in complex and resource-constrained contexts.

My engineering background has taught me to approach problems analytically; my experience in consulting and mission-driven organizations has taught me that effective solutions also require understanding people, institutions, and context.

I am interested in technology for social impact, sustainable development, AI and data, systems thinking, and human-centered problem solving .

What I Value

Human-Centered Problem Solving

Solutions should respond to the needs, realities, and constraints of the people and organizations they are designed to serve.

Evidence & Data

I believe better information and analysis can lead to better decisions, stronger programs, and more effective development interventions.

Systems Thinking

Complex problems rarely have a single cause or solution. I look for connections between people, institutions, technology, and the wider system.

Responsible Technology

Technology is a tool, not an end in itself. Its value comes from how well it responds to real needs and creates meaningful outcomes.

Building Capacity

I value solutions that strengthen organizations and people, allowing impact to continue beyond a single project.

🌱 Development Consulting — Colombia

At Oportunidad Estratégica, I worked on development projects involving government, private organizations, academia, and civil society.

My projects included:

  • Bioeconomy & Territory Mission — scientific and technological surveillance, foresight, stakeholder engagement, and strategic roadmaps for Colombia's bioeconomy policy.
  • INVIMA Institutional Strengthening — participatory diagnosis, international benchmarking, and recommendations for regulatory improvement.
  • Higher Education Financial Sustainability — applied research and analysis for ASCUN.
  • ICC World Chambers Federation — participatory strategic planning and impact-network analysis.
  • Strategic planning and development projects related to social security, energy access, SMEs, education, and sustainability .

These experiences taught me to work across disciplines and translate research, data, and stakeholder perspectives into development strategies.

📊 Data & Performance — New York Sun Works

At New York Sun Works, I developed Business Intelligence solutions to improve the organization's performance monitoring and operations.

I designed dynamic dashboards, developed metrics, cleaned and structured data, and created workflows for data collection and reporting.

The work supported an organization serving approximately 320 schools, 1,400 teachers, and 140,000 K–12 students annually , helping improve resource allocation, program monitoring, and impact assessment.

⚙️ Nonprofit Operations — Association House of Chicago

At Association House of Chicago, I worked on financial and operational processes supporting programs for underserved communities.

I redesigned workflows, developed financial reporting systems, and created automations to improve organizational efficiency and capacity.

This experience reinforced my belief that improving the systems behind a nonprofit can increase the organization's ability to deliver its social mission.

🤝 Community & Experiential Education

My commitment to social impact also comes from experiences outside formal employment.

Through Soñar Despierto, Off Bound Adventures, Proyecto Vida, and Progresa Fenicia , I worked with vulnerable children and youth, rural communities, and communities experiencing social and economic challenges.

These experiences shaped the way I understand development: solutions must be grounded in the realities of the communities they seek to serve.
